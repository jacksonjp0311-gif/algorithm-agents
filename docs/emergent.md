# Emergent intelligence

Emergent is a governed research layer over canonical knowledge.

1. A human or model proposes a private hypothesis with evidence and canonical node references.
2. Challengers attach support, counterexamples, or uncertainty.
3. A human may mark the hypothesis reviewed or rejected.
4. A reviewed hypothesis still does not alter canon.
5. A separate evidence-backed relationship proposal and human relationship review are required to change the canonical Nexus.

Research logs follow a separate private-to-public review. A public log is a field note, not canonical truth.
